# Conditional relativistic completion protocol

November 8, 2026. Exploratory / Not Promoted. Written before the checks in this directory.

Inputs are a chosen spin-half internal doublet, canonical quantization, an assigned positive mass m and free relativistic dispersion E=sqrt(p^2+m^2). These are conditional inputs, not conclusions of the SEFI action. Do not replace the existing worldline equations or claim their energy problem is repaired.

On flat momentum measure, test J=-i p cross derivative+S and a boost K=half anticommutator(X,E)+f(E) S cross p, with X=i derivative and S=Pauli/2. Determine the coefficient required by the boost commutator and regularity at p=0. Verify all 45 independent Poincare generator commutators as differential-operator identities on smooth compactly supported momentum spinors. Check mass and Pauli–Lubanski Casimirs and the formal Hermiticity condition. A symbolic commutator alone is not a self-adjointness proof; relate the operators to finite induced unitary transformations.

Separately test positive-energy Dirac projector algebra and both symmetric and antisymmetric two-copy tensor spaces. State explicitly whether exchange statistics follows from one-particle symmetry alone. Local free Dirac field quantization, if discussed, is a conventional reference construction with its extra locality and CAR assumptions, not a derived SEFI field.

Exact symbolic identities are the acceptance criterion. Keep failed candidates and sign/measure conventions visible. No physical mass fitting, electromagnetic coupling, interaction or experimental data is included.

Finite-transform check, declared before its execution: use canonical SL(2,C) boosts at masses 0.3,1,5 and deterministic momentum/rapidity samples with seed 20261108. Test mass-shell Lorentz mapping, SU(2) Wigner unitarity/determinant and composition, relative tolerance 2e-12. Compare the infinitesimal Wigner spin matrix with the boost spin term by a centered difference at step 1e-6, absolute tolerance 1e-8. These samples check implementation; the exact cocycle and invariant-measure argument in the note establish the finite unitary construction.
