# Can SEFI-inspired dynamics reduce computing resources?

Initial research brief and pilot report · 4 November 2026

**Research lead and originating framework author: Jason Duran Dutton. Status: Exploratory / Not Promoted.** Codex assisted with source discovery, targeted mathematical review, experimental design, code, execution and this draft. Jason's review and independent faculty review are pending. These roles do not imply that Jason has approved every new statement or that AI assistance constitutes independent peer review.

## Finding

The accessible work supports a precise investigation of coupled dynamical computing, but does not establish a hardware or energy advantage. The smallest equation-grounded pilot completed here solved 120 structured convex quadratic problems to the declared accuracy. Its simulated damped dynamics were slower than both conventional baselines. The paired median runtime ratio against the fastest correct baseline ranged from about 1,536 to 13,555 across tested configurations. This rejects the predeclared software-advantage criterion for this implementation and workload. No electrical energy or physical device performance was measured.

This is a useful negative result: the mathematical connection to optimization is explicit, conventional structure was accounted for, and the proposed advantage did not appear. It neither validates the wider physical interpretation nor rules out different algorithms or devices. It does not support a claim that data centers can be replaced or materially reduced.

## Source review and version distinctions

This was a targeted review of accessible mathematical texts, selected source modules, saved audit records and repository metadata, not a complete audit of every historical artifact. The V: drive was inaccessible. The author authorized using accessible sources, especially GitHub. Repository revisions, reviewed paths and local source hashes accompany this package. Sources were read before selecting the benchmark. Historical numerical claims below are attributed to their saved reports; they were not all rerun here.

**SEFI formal construction.** The scalar F=O+A+S+W and invariance under allowed transformations define a framework. The layer functions, admissible transformations and physical observable maps remain unspecified. An invariance statement is not automatically a conservation law along a solution. A compact formula does not provide free precision, storage, accessible information or computation. The numerical acceleration constraint is a stated condition; it is not derived for the spacetime trajectory merely by bounding internal coordinates. [Pinned source](https://github.com/JasonSEFIDEFI/SEFI-Manuscripts/blob/72ca952eaddf608b006ebaaa4615a422e0532ff9/sefi/sefi.tex).

**DEFI.** The vector identity coordinates are an explicit extension of scalar SEFI. With consistent metric conventions, variation of the prescribed-geometry identity sector gives coupled second-order oscillator equations. The isolated Origin energy is conserved because I_O''+I_O=0. That conserved energy does not identify the complete state, encode a solution, imply attraction or bound other coordinates. The older manuscript's implication from zero acceleration to invariance of the entire derivative/geometric tuple fails: I_O=sin(t) has zero second derivative but nonzero third derivative at t=0. Freezing nonzero identity coordinates also leaves geometry-dependent coupling. The later foundations/phase treatments acknowledge these limits. [Older pinned DEFI](https://github.com/JasonSEFIDEFI/SEFI-Manuscripts/blob/72ca952eaddf608b006ebaaa4615a422e0532ff9/DEFI/DEFI_Formal_Construction.tex).

**Phase dynamics.** The accessible November 3 local text gives the most direct computing substrate: mass-normalized fixed-geometry dynamics X''+BX=0 with eigenvalues 1-r,1,1+r. Positive definiteness requires r<1; r=1 permits drift; r>1 admits growth. Positive instantaneous stiffness under periodic forcing is insufficient for bounded evolution. Conditional phase lifting needs nonzero amplitude, orientation, observable quadratures and a cycle record. Reversing an oscillator orientation does not compute a past answer or make future boundary data available. Persistence, recurrence, reconstruction and attraction are distinct. The pilot independently checks the spectrum numerically; the paper's full Floquet and nonlinear claims were not newly proved here.

**GWFM versions differ materially.** GitHub main's gwfm.tex still contains the earlier proposal that positive worldline occupation and overlap reproduce an electron field and interference. That equivalence is not established by those definitions. The October 29 revised manuscript, available in the November 3 local work, explicitly adds complex amplitudes, unitary transitions and Born readout as assumptions. Its finite history sum equals the matrix product by expansion, and interference comes from amplitude cross terms. This is an explicit mathematical construction, not an extraction of quantum state space from bare curves. Its classical reconstruction theorem also bounds finite-trajectory approximation under its metric, speed and density assumptions; the exact circle example has error 1/(4N). These are resource limits rather than evidence of unlimited information in one entity. Spinor/electron-field reconstruction remains open. Both versions are retained separately in the synchronization record; they must not be conflated.

**Newer research changes the physical assessment.** The [Research status](https://github.com/JasonSEFIDEFI/Research/blob/86686396a7612f4b80c7225798db48af519353b6/RESEARCH_STATUS.md) records an explicit coordinate-time Hamiltonian obstruction for the unrestricted nonzero-curvature-coupled worldline realization. This is a bounded mathematical obstruction to a globally lower-bounded energy interpretation, not a disproof of all SEFI models. Its derivation was inspected as an existing research record, not independently reproduced in this pilot. The separate canonical two-field localized-structure calculations are a different model; no collective-coordinate derivation yet makes their persistence equivalent to the DEFI identity coordinates. Existing localized branches and critical-limit corrections remain on [draft SEFI-PY PR 6](https://github.com/JasonSEFIDEFI/SEFI-PY/pull/6), not promoted or merged by this work.

## Two research directions

**Software on existing computers.** A possible mechanism would be fewer operations, reduced data movement, lower required precision, reusable state between related inputs or better success probability at a fixed budget. Each requires an explicit input-to-answer map and a matched conventional comparison. Simulating dynamics is itself conventional computation and includes state storage, integration error and stopping checks. Phase alone is not an additional computational resource. A persistent configuration may permit warm starts, but conventional methods must receive the same previous data/state and amortization opportunity.

**Physical implementation.** A possible mechanism would be parallel local interactions in a medium implementing a useful computation with lower total programming, evolution and readout cost. First reproduce a specified effective equation in a calibrated device; then compare that device with an optimized digital implementation and an ordinary analog device implementing the same equation. Matching classical oscillator dynamics would validate the implementation, not uniquely establish the SEFI ontology. A new SEFI-specific physical claim needs an independently calibrated, distinguishable prediction.

Electronic, mechanical or photonic implementations are candidates to assess after selecting a constitutive mapping, not devices established by the current code. The inspected Voxel compiler computes phases and the serial interface sends float frames. Neither supplies calibrated plant dynamics, measured power, feedback/correction or successful optimization. The unrestricted full geometric action is not used as a stable hardware blueprint in this pilot.

## Why this pilot

We selected solving Bx=u, equivalently minimizing f(x)=xᵀBx/2-uᵀx, on the actual three-coordinate linear reduction. This has a direct equation-level connection. A phase Max-Cut pilot would require an additional binary encoding, phase-binarization interaction, annealing schedule and rounding rule not derived in the supplied reduction. Existing oscillator Ising work already illustrates why readout/rounding and hardware overhead must be specified; see [Wang and Roychowdhury](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.08102) and [Erementchouk, Shukla and Mazumder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07591). This is not a claim of algorithmic novelty.

The task is deliberately easy for a conventional solver. Avoiding its available closed-form block solution would manufacture a misleading comparison. Scaling batches of independent blocks tests throughput and implementation overhead, not increasing interaction complexity or hard optimization scaling.

The full definition, resource boundaries and decisions were saved in **PROTOCOL.md before comparisons**. It specifies encoding, forcing, zero initialization, RK4 evolution, damping, stopping, readout, baselines, seeds, tolerances, timing boundaries and failure criteria. A wording error in its seed-range prose is corrected here: the executable seeds range from 11001–11010 to 22001–22010, assigned by 11001+3000*size_index+1000*radius_index+case_index. Raw results contain every seed. The original protocol is retained unchanged so its recorded hash remains verifiable. No numerical design changed after seeing results. This is a local frozen protocol, not an external preregistration.

## Completed comparison

Four sizes (3,30,300,3000 variables), three coupling bounds (0.5,0.9,0.99), ten independently generated instances per setting, three timed repeats per method: 120 problems and 1,080 timed solves. All three methods met residual ≤10⁻⁶ and forward error ≤10⁻³ on all main cases. Algebraic maximum relative residual was 1.65×10⁻¹⁵, CG 9.85×10⁻⁷ and oscillator 9.75×10⁻⁷. CG is a standard SPD baseline; [Netlib's Templates](https://www.netlib.org/templates/templates.html) documents its scope and operation structure. This implementation is a transparent NumPy baseline, not a claim to represent the fastest available library.

Representative largest-size results (host seconds, medians across case-level repeat medians):

| Coupling bound | Algebraic | CG | Dynamics | Paired dynamics/best ratio, 95% bootstrap interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.50 | 0.000101 | 0.00247 | 0.159 | 1,570 [1,533,1,705] |
| 0.90 | 0.000104 | 0.00656 | 0.478 | 4,564 [4,034,5,336] |
| 0.99 | 0.000103 | 0.0225 | 1.396 | 13,555 [11,068,15,869] |

These ratios are medians of paired ratios, not ratios of the displayed medians. On this host, dynamics needed median 210,456.5,1414 steps respectively at the largest size; CG needed 11,29,87 iterations. RK4 uses four force/operator evaluations plus one residual evaluation per step. Smaller eigenvalues and reduced damping increased settling cost. Near-threshold coupling supplies no free acceleration. The observed large runtime gap includes Python/NumPy overhead and is implementation-specific; it is not a hardware lower bound.

Verification checked every saved method-case result, block multiplication against explicit matrices, closed-form solutions against an independent batched dense solver on 40 new instances, and RK4 against an analytic damped scalar solution. Halving the verification step reduced fixed-time position errors from 1.42×10⁻⁵ to 8.37×10⁻⁷ to 5.07×10⁻⁸, consistent with fourth-order convergence. A separate undamped analytic control is bounded but does not converge to the minimizer. The main half-step diagnostics also met the final tolerance; they do not establish uniform trajectory accuracy for all cases.

Three selected 30-variable sensitivity cases passed at float32. Rounding couplings at 8,12,16 fractional bits produced original-input residuals approximately 1.6–1.9×10⁻³, 1.1–1.3×10⁻⁴ and 6.7–8.5×10⁻⁶, all above target. Added readout noise of standard deviation 10⁻⁶ produced residuals 1.3–1.5×10⁻⁶. These are illustrative probes, not device failure probabilities or full dynamical noise tests. The noisy/rounded result is not silently corrected.

## Resource accounting and limits

Preparation and independent reference time are recorded separately. Per-method copies, zeroing, arithmetic, convergence checks and returned arrays are included in solve timing. Imports, process startup, case generation and reference validation are excluded from ratios and must be added/amortized in a deployed system. Reference solutions are used only for offline evaluation, not for evolution or stopping. Shared generation is a synthetic test cost; real parsing/programming must be measured separately.

Coefficients occupy 2n/3 float64 values and forcing n values, about 13.3n bytes combined. Dynamics require at least another 2n values for x and v, plus RK stages and temporary arrays. CG uses solution, residual, direction and work arrays. The algebraic method needs one output array plus temporaries. These are logical storage counts, not measured peak resident memory; array copies and interpreter/library memory remain. Precision is finite throughout; an analog coordinate cannot be credited with infinitely many reliably readable bits.

All operator communication is local within blocks; no network communication was used or measured. Global norm checks and CG reductions require data movement even on a single host; they would have communication costs on distributed or analog systems. Hardware readout is modeled only as an output array and a small sensitivity probe. No DAC/ADC cost, calibration drift, stochastic force noise, correction/retry cost, physical reset or holding power was measured. This is why no physical energy conclusion follows from host timing or the dimensionless dynamical energy.

For physical experiments use the protocol's complete energy ledger, including controller, memory, conversion, measurement, verification and failed attempts. Calibrate a physical time scale before converting simulated duration. Damping dissipates energy; stability alone does not say how many joules a solution costs. Measure both incremental and total wall energy, and declare amortization/reuse rather than selectively subtracting common overhead.

## Missing assumptions and decisive next work

1. Select the authoritative manuscript versions and reconcile older claims with the phase/foundations corrections. Preserve earlier files as history.
2. Define a useful target workload and instance distribution, required accuracy and service constraints. This batch linear solve is a diagnostic, not evidence of a major data-center workload opportunity.
3. Derive any new multi-coordinate network, nonlinear interaction or phase/binary encoding from a stated action or declare it as an engineering extension. Establish stability, attraction and readable outputs under noise. Do not transfer full-action stability claims from the fixed-geometry reduction.
4. Specify a physical coordinate/coupler mapping, admissible signed gains, units, dynamic range, time scale, thermal noise, drift, preparation and reset. Relate identity/geometry variables to independently measurable quantities or keep the model purely algorithmic.
5. Establish whether persistence enables useful reuse across related inputs, with equal-history warm-start conventional controls. Include information retention, refresh/holding cost and correction. Novelty and practical relevance remain to be shown.
6. Measure end-to-end energy, latency, throughput, area/memory and reliability at matched output quality. Include optimized CPU/GPU and ordinary analog alternatives where relevant. No choice of a physical platform is justified by this pilot alone.

## Practical plan with faculty and students

**Immediate review checkpoint:** Jason reviews the source/version map and the pilot's interpretation, selects the claim to retain and invites faculty review of the equations and experimental fairness. Keep the negative result. Independent reproduction should use the committed protocol, script, environment record and raw results; record any departures and preserve the original run.

**Student-sized work packages:** one mathematical project can independently derive the spectrum, damped convergence and precision sensitivity; one numerical project can reproduce results and compare compiled/optimized methods; one instrumentation project can develop a calibrated three-channel oscillator model and power/measurement budget. These are proposed collaboration roles, not assigned or completed contributions. Faculty reviewers should be free to recommend changes or rejection. No invitations were sent by this work.

**Next implementation gate:** do not enlarge this block benchmark merely to seek a positive result. First choose between a defined warm-start/reuse question or an interconnected quadratic family with a derived coupling map. Freeze a new protocol with tuned-on-training-only parameters, unseen distributions, negative controls and ablations. Add forcing/damping ablations and compare identical extensions under ordinary dynamical-systems terminology. Stop the advantage claim if the baseline matches or exceeds performance, the benefit disappears after preparation/readout, or required precision defeats the hardware budget.

**Device gate:** only after the model predicts a credible implementation benefit, build one small calibrated realization and test independently predicted impulse responses and settling/readout quality. Then measure full energy per successful answer. If conventional oscillator physics explains the device equally well, report that equivalence explicitly.

**Infrastructure gate:** characterize a real workload fraction, throughput and latency, utilization, redundancy, availability, failure/retry costs, host/offload traffic, manufacturing/resource cost and cooling effects. Even an energy advantage for one kernel need not reduce installed servers or peak capacity. An ideal fraction-f workload accelerated by s reduces total work at most f(1-1/s) before overhead. Facility-demand claims need measured adoption and capacity evidence beyond any small benchmark.

## Reproduction and contributions

Run `python benchmark.py`, then `python verify.py` with Python and NumPy (recorded run: Python 3.14.7, NumPy 2.5.2, Windows 11, Intel Family 6 Model 122, requested one numerical-library thread). Timings will vary. The saved results identify environment, seeds, raw repeats and protocol/script SHA-256 hashes. The uncertainty intervals cover cases on this host and do not incorporate cross-host or physical-system uncertainty.
